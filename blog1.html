44Studios

"Inspired by society’s wonders and contradictions, 44Studios was conceived in 2013 with the idea of creating something anyone could use to truly capture the day. With limited resources, years were spent cycling through mock-ups — building, refining, and sometimes tearing everything down — in pursuit of that vision."

"In the world I imagine for 2044, I see the creation of an effortless ecosystem — one infused with spiritual energy, offering a timeless guide to navigating life with grace through both joy and sorrow."

"BLITZ"

Blitz embodies multiple meanings, reflecting the energy, urgency, and teamwork required to achieve the mission — an energy that can propel individuals to reach unforeseen heights.

"FORTYFOUR"

‘44’ reflects guidance, balance, and alignment — an angel number I’ve noticed repeating in the world around me, a subtle reminder of purpose and direction.
